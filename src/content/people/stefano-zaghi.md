Stefano Zaghi is a research scientist at the CNR Institute for Applied Mathematics (IAC) in Rome. He holds a PhD in
Aerospace Engineering from Sapienza University of Rome (2009), with a thesis on the unsteady gas dynamics of
solid-rocket-motor igniter jets.

From 2008 to 2022 he worked in the CFD department of CNR-INM (formerly INSEAN) on high-performance solvers for ship
hydrodynamics, submarine manoeuvring and wind-turbine aerodynamics, taking part in national and international research
projects, some of which he managed.

He leads ADAM, a GPU-accelerated adaptive-mesh-refinement framework, and its solvers FLUME and PRISM, and has held
several supercomputing grants as principal investigator. He is a long-standing author of open-source modern-Fortran
software, including FoBiS, FLAP, VTKFortran and StringiFor.
